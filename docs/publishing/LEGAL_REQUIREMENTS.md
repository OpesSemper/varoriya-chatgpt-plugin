# Legal, privacy, and publisher requirements

This file is an engineering gate, not legal advice and not a privacy policy or terms of service.

Before public submission, an authorized publisher and legal/privacy owner must approve:

- the exact legal entity/developer name shown to users;
- ownership or authorized use of the public domain, Varoriya name, logos, screenshots, and marketing claims;
- a public privacy policy describing controller/processor roles, data categories, purposes, legal bases where applicable, recipients/providers, international transfers, retention, deletion, security, children/minors, and contact/rights handling;
- public terms covering acceptable use, user content rights, generated content, prohibited content, credit/pricing behavior, refunds/cancellations, availability, warranty/liability, termination, and governing terms;
- a support process for account access, deletion, billing disputes, security reports, and abuse reports;
- reviewer credentials and test data that contain no personal or confidential information;
- regional availability and age/consent restrictions;
- retention periods for ownership, quote, cost, idempotency, logs, backups, and incident evidence;
- subprocessors and Varoriya/provider contractual authority for the intended data flow.

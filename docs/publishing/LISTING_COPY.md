## Long description

Varoriya Generate connects ChatGPT to a publisher-operated MCP gateway for image, video, and audio generation. It helps users compare currently supported models, request an exact quote, review the price and material parameters, explicitly approve a credit-consuming action, submit with idempotency protection, poll job status, and retrieve expiring results. Protected tools require OAuth and enforce account scopes and ownership boundaries.

The service does not generate automatically from an estimate or an earlier confirmation. Changing model, media kind, dimensions, duration, quality, input files, or other material parameters requires a new quote and confirmation.

## Example prompts

- Show me current image models and quote a 1024 × 1024 image; do not generate until I confirm.
- Compare supported video models for a short product clip and show the live credit price.
- Check the status of my Varoriya job and tell me when its download link expires.
- Show my balance, then help me choose a lower-cost audio model.
